Henry Francis Lyte (1793–1847) was a Scottish Anglican hymnist and poet. After studying at Trinity College, Dublin, Lyte took Anglican holy orders in 1815 and for some time he held a curacy in Taghmon near Wexford, Ireland. He wrote many famous hymns including ["Praise, My Soul, the King of Heaven"](/liturgy/music/hymns/praise-my-soul-the-king-of-heaven).
