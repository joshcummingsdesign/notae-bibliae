Within the Anglican tradition, "divines" are clergy of the Church of England whose theological writings have been considered standards for faith, doctrine, and worship.

* [Thomas Cranmer](/people/anglican-divines/thomas-cranmer) (1489–1556)
* [Richard Hooker](/people/anglican-divines/richard-hooker) (1554–1600)
* [Lancelot Andrewes](/people/anglican-divines/lancelot-andrewes) (1555–1626)
* [Thomas Ken](/people/anglican-divines/thomas-ken) (1637–1711)
