**Archangel**

[St. Gabriel](/glossary/names#gabriel) is one of the holy angels who stand before God (Rev. 8:2) and is especially known as the bearer of divine messages. He appeared to the prophet Daniel to explain the meaning of his visions and reveal things that were yet to come (Dan. 8:16). In the Gospel, Gabriel was sent to the priest [Zechariah](/people/saints/biblical/zechariah-the-priest) to announce the birth of [St. John the Baptist](/people/saints/biblical/john-the-baptist) (Luke 1:19), and six months later was sent by God to the [Blessed Virgin Mary](/people/saints/biblical/the-blessed-virgin-mary) at Nazareth, announcing that she would conceive by the Holy Spirit and bear Jesus, the Son of the Highest (Luke 1:26-27). Thus Gabriel is especially associated with the revelation of God's purposes and, above all, with the announcement of the Incarnation.
