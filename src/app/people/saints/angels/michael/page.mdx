**Archangel**

[St. Michael](/glossary/names#michael) is the chief of the angelic guardians and prince of the heavenly host. Holy Scripture shows him contending against evil, first as the prince appointed to defend the people of Israel (Dan. 10:13), and again as the leader of the heavenly armies who fights against Satan (Rev. 12:7). As the angels are guardians and ministers of God's people, Michael is especially regarded as the guardian of the Church, standing at the head of the angelic host in its defence against the powers of evil. He is also numbered among the seven holy angels who stand before God (Rev. 8:2).
