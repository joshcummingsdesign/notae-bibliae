**1226 AD**

[St. Francis of Assisi](/glossary/names#francis) was born in 1182 to Pietro di Bernardone, a wealthy merchant, and spent his youth preparing to follow his father's trade. After imprisonment at Perugia and an illness at Spoleto, he gradually turned from his former life to prayer, charity, and the service of God, restoring the ruined churches of San Damiano, St. Peter, and Santa Maria degli Angeli at the Portiuncula. In 1208, hearing Christ's command to his apostles to take neither money nor provisions for their journey (Matt. 10:7-10), Francis embraced a life of absolute poverty and began preaching the kingdom of heaven; disciples soon gathered around him, forming the community that became the Franciscan Order. Renowned for his humility, tenderness, and love for all God's creatures, he sought above all to imitate Christ in poverty and simplicity. In 1223 his *Rule* received papal approval, and that Christmas he arranged at Greccio a representation of the manger at Bethlehem. The following year, while praying on Mount La Verna, Francis received the <Definition lang="greek" anchor="stigmata" text="stigmata" /> of Christ. His health thereafter declined, and he died at the Portiuncula on October 3, 1226, and was buried in the Church of San Giorgio in Assisi on the following day.
