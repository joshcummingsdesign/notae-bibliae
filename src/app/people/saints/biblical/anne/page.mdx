**Mother of the Blessed Virgin Mary** (c. 4 AD)

[St. Anne](/glossary/names#anne), the wife of [St. Joachim](/people/saints/biblical/joachim) and mother of the [Blessed Virgin Mary](/people/saints/biblical/the-blessed-virgin-mary), was a righteous descendant of David who, according to tradition, remained childless until God answered her prayers and granted her a daughter, just as the barren Hannah had received Samuel by prayer and divine promise (1 Sam. 1:2). [St. John of Damascus](/people/saints/greek-fathers/john-of-damascus) praises Saints Joachim and Anne as a holy and virtuous couple whose devout life prepared them to become the parents of the Mother of God, through whom the promises made to David would find their fulfillment in Christ. Their daughter Mary was dedicated to God, brought up in the temple, and prepared in holiness to receive the Son of God into her womb. St. Anne is therefore remembered especially for her faithfulness and as the mother of the Blessed Virgin, through whom she became an earthly ancestor of the Incarnate Lord.
