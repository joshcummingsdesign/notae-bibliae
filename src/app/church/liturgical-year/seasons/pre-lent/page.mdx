**Septuagesima Sunday – Lent (exclusive)**

<Definition lang="english" anchor="lenten" text="Pre-Lent" /> is the season encompassing the period of time from Septuagesima Sunday leading up to [Lent](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/lent). It traditionally opens a period of religious instruction leading to the reception of catechumens at Easter. The season focuses on examination of conscience and repentance before the Lenten fast.

## Observances

* **Third Sunday Before Lent** — [Septuagesima Sunday](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/pre-lent/septuagesima-sunday)
* **Second Sunday Before Lent** — [Sexagesima Sunday](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/pre-lent/sexagesima-sunday)
* **Sunday Next Before Lent** — [Quinquagesima Sunday](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/pre-lent/quinquagesima-sunday)
