<Initial text="Quinquagesima Sunday" />

**Sunday Next Before Lent**<br/>
*Principal Sunday*

<Definition lang="latin" anchor="quinquagesima" text="Quinquagesima" /> comes from the Latin for 50th, as it falls around 50 days before Easter. It is observed on the Sunday Next Before Lent. It is a time marked by spiritual preparation for the season of Lent.
