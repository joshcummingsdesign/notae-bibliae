**Passion Sunday – Holy Saturday (inclusive)**

Passiontide (<Definition lang="latin" anchor="passio" text="Passion" /> + -<Definition lang="english" anchor="tide" text="tide" />) is the season encompassing the last two weeks of [Lent](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/lent). It starts with the Fifth Sunday in Lent, which is called Passion Sunday. It goes through Holy Week, and ends on Holy Saturday.
