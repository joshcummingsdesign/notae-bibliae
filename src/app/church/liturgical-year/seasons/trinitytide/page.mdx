**Trinity Sunday – Advent (exclusive)**

Trinitytide (Trinity + -<Definition lang="english" anchor="tide" text="tide" />) is the season encompassing the period of time from Trinity Sunday leading up to [Advent](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/advent).

## Observances

* **First Sunday After Pentecost** — [Trinity Sunday](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/trinitytide/trinity-sunday)
* **Thursday After Trinity Sunday** — [Corpus Christi](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/trinitytide/corpus-christi)
* **July 2** — [The Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/trinitytide/visitation)
* **August 6** — [The Transfiguration of Christ](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/trinitytide/transfiguration)
* **August 7** — [The Holy Name of Jesus](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/trinitytide/holy-name)
* **September 14** — [The Exaltation of the Holy Cross](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/trinitytide/holy-cross)
* **Wednesday After Holy Cross** — [Ember Days in Trinitytide](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/trinitytide/ember-days-in-trinitytide)
* **October 31** — [Allhallowtide](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/trinitytide/allhallowtide)
