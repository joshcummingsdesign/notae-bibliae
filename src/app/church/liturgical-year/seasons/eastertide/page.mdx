**Easter – Pentecost (exclusive)**

Eastertide (<Definition lang="english" anchor="easter" text="Easter" /> + -<Definition lang="english" anchor="tide" text="tide" />) is the season encompassing the 50 days from Easter to [Pentecost](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/whitsuntide/pentecost).

## Observances

* **Sundown on Holy Saturday** — [Easter Vigil](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/eastertide/easter-vigil)
* **Sunday on or After the Paschal Full Moon** — [Easter Day](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/eastertide/easter-day)
* **First Sunday After Easter** — [Low Sunday](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/eastertide/low-sunday)
* **Fifth Sunday After Easter** — [Rogation Sunday](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/eastertide/rogation-days)
* **The Three Days Before Ascension Day** — [Rogation Days](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/eastertide/rogation-days)
* **Forty Days After Easter** — [Ascensiontide](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/eastertide/ascensiontide)
