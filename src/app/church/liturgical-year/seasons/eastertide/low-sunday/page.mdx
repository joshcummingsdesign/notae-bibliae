**First Sunday After Easter**<br/>
*Principal Feast*

Low Sunday, also called the Second Sunday of Easter, is so named because of the contrast between it and the great Easter festival immediately preceding.

Thomas, doubting and perhaps believing they had seen a spirit, says that unless he touches Jesus' hands and feels the mark of the nails, he will never believe (John 20:24-25). Eight days later (inclusive), the Sunday after Jesus' Resurrection, the eleven disciples are gathered with the doors locked. Jesus miraculously opens the doors and stands among them. Thomas believes (John 20:26-28). Jesus is then with the disciples for 40 days before he ascends into heaven (Acts 1:3).
