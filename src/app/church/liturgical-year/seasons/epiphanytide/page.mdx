**Epiphany – Pre-Lent (exclusive)**

Epiphanytide (<Definition lang="greek" anchor="epiphaneia" text="Epiphany" /> + -<Definition lang="english" anchor="tide" text="tide" />) is the season encompassing the period of time from Epiphany leading up to [Pre-Lent](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/pre-lent).

## Observances

* **January 6** — [The Epiphany](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/epiphanytide/epiphany)
* **February 2** — [The Purification of the Blessed Virgin Mary](/church/liturgical-year/seasons/epiphanytide/purification)
