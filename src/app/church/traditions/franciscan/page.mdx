<Initial text="Franciscan" />

The tradition rooted in the monastic practices of [St. Francis of Assisi](/people/saints/medieval/francis-of-assisi). The Franciscans have three orders. The First Order, or Order of Friars Minor, refers to a [medicant order](/glossary/historical-terms#mendicant-orders) of men who have taken religious vows. The Second Order, or [Order of Poor Clares](/people/saints/medieval/clare-of-assisi), refers to women who have taken religious vows. The Third Order refers to laypersons who remain in secular society, living out the ideals of the Order. Franciscan friars traditionally wear the grey habit.
