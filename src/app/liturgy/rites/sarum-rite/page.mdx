<Definition lang="english" anchor="sarum" text="The Sarum Rite" />, or <Definition lang="latin" anchor="usus" text="Use of Sarum" />, was the local form of the Latin liturgy developed at Salisbury Cathedral under [St. Osmund](/people/saints/medieval/osmund-of-salisbury) and his successors from the late 11th century until the English Reformation. As a local use of the [Roman Rite](/liturgy/rites/roman-rite), it incorporated distinctive English customs of ceremony, chant, and calendar. The Sarum Rite is significant because it preserves the liturgical tradition of the medieval English Church before the Reformation.
