## Matins

---

### Silent Prayers

- [Sign of the Cross](/liturgy/prayers/sign-of-the-cross)

<Poetry>
|  Open thou my mouth, O Lord, to bless thy Name:
|      cleanse also my heart from all vain thoughts,
|          that I may be worthy to be heard
|          before the face of thy divine Majesty.
|  Through Jesus Christ thy Son our Lord,
|      who liveth and reigneth with thee
|          in the unity of the Holy Ghost,
|          one God, world without end. Amen.
</Poetry>

- [Lord's Prayer](/liturgy/prayers/our-father)
- [Hail Mary](/liturgy/prayers/hail-mary)

- Versicles

  - [*Venite*](/liturgy/music/chants/venite) (with [antiphon](/glossary/liturgical-terms#antiphon))

**1–3 Nocturns**
- Psalms (with antiphons and *Gloria*)
- [Versicle](/glossary/liturgical-terms#versicle)
- Lord's Prayer and Hail Mary (silently)
- Benediction
- Lessons (three, with [responsories](/glossary/liturgical-terms#responsory))

### Concluding Rites

- [*Te Deum*](/liturgy/music/chants/te-deum)*
- Versicle

## Seasonal Considerations

---

**Te Deum**
- **All Feasts and Lord's Days**
- **Daily during:**
  - Christmastide
  - Octave of Epiphany
  - Eastertide, except on Rogation Days
  - Whitsuntide, except on Ember Days
- **Omitted during:**
  - Advent
  - The Holy Innocents, unless it falls on a Sunday
  - From Septuagesima to Easter (exclusive)
  - All Souls' Day[^3][^4]
