## Matins

<h3><Red large={true} text="❦ Opening Rites" /></h3>

**Opening Versicle**
- [*Domine, labia mea*](/liturgy/music/chants/domine-labia-mea) (x3)

**Opening Psalm**
- Psalm 3 (with [*Gloria*](/liturgy/music/chants/gloria-patri))[^1]

**Invitatory**
- [*Venite*](/liturgy/music/chants/venite) (with antiphon)

**Hymn**
- [Ambrosian hymn](/glossary/liturgical-terms#ambrosian-hymns)

<h3><Red large={true} text="❦ Office Proper (Lord's Days)" /></h3>

**First Nocturn**
- Psalms (six, with [antiphons](/glossary/liturgical-terms#antiphon))
- [Versicle](/glossary/liturgical-terms#versicle)
- Abbot's Blessing
- Lessons (four, with [responsories](/glossary/liturgical-terms#responsory))[^2]
- *Gloria*[^3]

**Second Nocturn**
- Psalms (six, with antiphons)
- Versicle
- Lessons (four, with responsories)
- *Gloria*

**Third Nocturn**
- [Canticles](/glossary/liturgical-terms#canticle) (three, from the Prophets, with [*Alleluia*](/liturgy/music/chants/alleluia))[^4]
- Versicle
- Abbot's Blessing
- Lessons (four, from the New Testament, with responsories)
- *Gloria*

**Concluding Rites**
- [*Te Deum*](/liturgy/music/chants/te-deum)
- Gospel Lesson[^5]
- [*Te decet laus*](/liturgy/music/chants/te-decet-laus)
- [Lord’s Prayer](/liturgy/prayers/our-father)[^6]
- [Benediction](/glossary/liturgical-terms#benediction)

<h3><Red large={true} text="❦ Office Proper (Ferial Days)" /></h3>

**First Nocturn**
- Psalms (six, with antiphons)
- [Versicle](/glossary/liturgical-terms#versicle)
- Abbot's Blessing
- Lessons (three, with responsories)[^7]
- *Gloria*

**Second Nocturn**
- Psalms (six, with *Alleluia*)
- Lessons (from the Apostle, by heart)
- Versicle

**Concluding Rites**
- [*Kyrie*](/liturgy/music/chants/kyrie)
- Lord's Prayer

<h3><Red large={true} text="❦ After the Office" /></h3>

The period of time between Matins and Lauds may be used for thoughtful study of the Psalms and Lessons. This interval will be longer in the winter, when there is more time before dawn, and shorter in the summer.

---

## Lauds

**Opening Psalms**

- Psalm 67[^8]
- Psalm 51 (with antiphon)[^9]

**Office Proper**
- Psalms (two)
- [Old Testament Canticle](/liturgy/daily-office/psalter#old-testament-canticles)
- [Laudate Psalms](/glossary/liturgical-terms#laudate-psalms)
- Lesson (by heart, with responsory)
  - **S:** From the Book of Revelation
  - **M–Sat:** From the Apostle
- Hymn (Ambrosian hymn)
- Versicle
- Gospel Canticle[^10]

**Concluding Rites**
- *Kyrie*
- Lord's Prayer

**Opening Versicle**

**Opening Versicle**

- *Deus in adjutorium* (with *Gloria*)

**Office Proper**
