<Initial text="The Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary" />

**July 2**<br/>
*Feast*

The feast of The Visitation of the [Blessed Virgin Mary](/people/saints/biblical/the-blessed-virgin-mary) celebrates the visit of Mary, who was pregnant with Jesus, to [Elizabeth](/people/saints/biblical/elizabeth), who was pregnant with [St. John the Baptist](/people/saints/biblical/john-the-baptist).

After the angel [Gabriel](/people/saints/angels/gabriel) foretells to Mary the birth of Christ, Mary goes to the hill country of Judea (likely Hebron) to visit her relative Elizabeth, who is pregnant with John the Baptist. Baby John leaps in Elizabeth's womb and Elizabeth is filled with the Holy Spirit. She blesses Mary, and Mary prays the [Magnificat](/liturgy/music/chants/magnificat), which begins with, "My soul magnifies the Lord" (Luke 1:46). She thanks God for his grace and mercy, and for exalting the humble.

The [Franciscans](/people/saints/medieval/francis-of-assisi) have observed this feast since around 1263. In 1389, Pope Urban VI added The Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary to the Roman Calendar for celebration on July 2.
