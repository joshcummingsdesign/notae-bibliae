<Initial text="The Holy Name of Jesus" />

**August 7**<br/>
*Feast*

This feast honors the Name “Jesus,” given to Christ in accordance with the angel’s command to Joseph before his birth: “She will bear a son, and you shall call his name Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins” (Matt. 1:21). The devotion particularly emphasizes the theological meaning of the Name, and the salvation proclaimed by it (cf. Phil. 2:9-11). The feast of the Holy Name of Jesus has been celebrated in the Western Church since the end of the fifteenth century, and its observance was officially granted to the [Franciscans](/people/saints/medieval/francis-of-assisi) in 1530.
