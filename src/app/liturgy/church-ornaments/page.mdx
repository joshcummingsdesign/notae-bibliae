## Altar Candles

Altars typically have two white candles of pure wax upon them. Their light symbolizes Christ, the Light of the World (John 8:12); their number signifies his two natures, human and divine; and their color and purity reflect his holiness and purity.

## Censer

A vessel in which incense is burned over hot charcoal.

## Chalice

A chalice is a standing cup used to hold sacramental wine during the Eucharist.

## Crozier

A Crozier, from the French *croisier*, which means "one who bears a cross", is a staff which resembles a shepherd's crook, carried by a bishop or abbot, representing that he is a shepherd of the Church.

## Cruet

A small vessel used to hold wine or water for the Eucharist.

## Incense Boat

A small covered vessel that holds the incense before it is paced on the burning charcoal in the censer.

## Paten

The plate used to hold the host during the Eucharist.

## Thurible

A <Definition lang="latin" anchor="thuribulum" text="thurible" /> is a censer suspended by chains, which is swung to disperse the fragrant smoke as a visible symbol of prayer, reverence, purification, and God's presence.

## Verge

A <Definition lang="latin" anchor="virga" text="verge" /> is a wooden staff carried by the Verger in a procession.
