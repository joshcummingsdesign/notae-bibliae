## History

A [canticle](/glossary/liturgical-terms#canticle) from the verses immediately preceding the [*Benedicite*](/liturgy/music/chants/benedicite), which comes from the *Prayer of Azariah and Song of the Three Holy Children*, found in the Septuagint version of Daniel 3 and included in the Vulgate. Though mostly used in the Eastern Church, the *Benedictus es, Domine* was included in the 1928 American *Book of Common Prayer* as a shorter substitute for the [*Benedicite*](/liturgy/music/chants/benedicite).[^1]
