<Initial text="Dominican" />

The tradition rooted in the monastic practices of [St. Dominic](/people/saints/medieval/dominic-of-guzman). The Dominicans, or Order of Preachers, are a [medicant order](/glossary/historical-terms#mendicant-orders) consisting of three divisions. The First Order refers to men who have taken religious vows. The Second Order refers to women who have taken religious vows. The Third Order refers to laypersons who remain in secular society, living out the ideals of the Order. Dominican friars traditionally wear the white habit.
